About Eco-Cycle

Good technology deserves
a thoughtful next chapter.

We’re here to make responsible electronics reuse and recycling easier to understand and easier to act on.

Moving toward a circular future
Our purpose

Less waste.
More possibility.

Every year, useful devices are set aside while valuable materials and components are lost. Eco-Cycle exists to help people and organizations make more responsible choices throughout the life of their electronics.

We believe a better system starts with clear information, convenient collection, secure handling, and a preference for extending a device’s useful life whenever possible.

What guides us

Responsible at every step.

From the first collection to the final material recovery, good outcomes depend on thoughtful decisions all along the way.

Reuse before recycling

Keep working devices and components in use for as long as they can serve someone well.

Respect for your data

Handle devices with privacy in mind and make secure data management part of the conversation.

Care for our environment

Prioritize responsible processing to help keep hazardous materials out of landfills and ecosystems.
