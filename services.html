Eco-Cycle services

From collection to next life.

Practical, responsible options for devices at every stage—from useful equipment ready for reuse to end-of-life electronics.

01 / Collect

Electronics collection

Convenient collection options for households and organizations looking for a responsible way to move on from unwanted devices.

02 / Recover

Responsible recycling

End-of-life electronics are directed toward responsible processing and material recovery rather than general waste.

03 / Protect

Data-aware handling

We help you plan for privacy and secure data management when preparing devices for reuse, recycling, or retirement.

04 / Plan

Disposal consultation

Get practical guidance on responsible device handling, sorting, and choosing appropriate next steps for your equipment.

05 / Learn

Education & awareness

Clear information and learning opportunities that help communities understand the impact of e-waste and how to reduce it.

06 / Reuse

Refurbishment & reuse

Working devices can be assessed for repair, refurbishment, and a second useful life before recycling is considered.
